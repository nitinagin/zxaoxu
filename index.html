01 / ABOUT AOXU

关于奥旭About AOXU

ZHONGXIANG AOXU MINING CO., LTD.（钟祥市奥旭矿业有限公司）位于湖北省钟祥市。根据相关商业交易资料，公司作为采购方参与国际商品采购与跨境贸易，相关交易涉及液体糖浆等商品。ZHONGXIANG AOXU MINING CO., LTD. is located in Zhongxiang City, Hubei Province, China. Based on available commercial transaction documents, the company participates as a buyer in international commodity procurement and cross-border trade, including transactions involving liquid sugar syrup.
